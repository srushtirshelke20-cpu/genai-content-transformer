## 🎙️ Recording & Production Tips for Member 5
1. **Screen Recording:** Use **OBS Studio** or **Loom** (set resolution to 1080p, 60fps).
2. **Audio:** Use a clear microphone with background noise cancellation. Speak at an energetic, steady pace.
3. **PowerPoint Demo (1:20):** Make sure you have Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides open in the background so you can click the downloaded file and show it opening immediately!
