GLOBAL ENTERPRISE DIRECTIVE: WORKPLACE GENERATIVE AI GOVERNANCE POLICY

Effective Date: October 1, 2026
Audience: All Global Full-Time Employees and Contractors

BACKGROUND:
As artificial intelligence tools become embedded in daily workflows, our organization must balance productivity acceleration with the legal imperative to protect client confidential data, proprietary source code, and intellectual property.

KEY POLICY MANDATES:
1. Approved Tooling: Employees may only use enterprise-licensed AI platforms with data isolation agreements. Public, consumer-tier LLM subscriptions are strictly forbidden for corporate work.
2. Prohibited Data Uploads: Under no circumstances should source code, internal financial balances, customer PII, or unreleased product designs be pasted into external AI interfaces.
3. Code & Content Verification: Any AI-generated code, legal drafts, or client communications must undergo human peer review before deployment or publication.
4. Accountability: The human author remains 100% legally and professionally responsible for any hallucinations, copyright infringements, or inaccuracies produced by AI tools.

COMPLIANCE & AUDITING:
Automated DLP (Data Loss Prevention) sensors will monitor outgoing network traffic. Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action up to immediate contract termination.
